Stand films · exhibition motion

Films for the show floor.

Silent films for exhibition stands: a product built part by part, a cutaway, a photograph brought to life, or light across a ribbon of screen. Each one loops, and each is made for the screen you have.

Product build · 30 seconds

Built part by part

337 parts, one pump. The product is modelled part by part and assembled on screen: the parts laid out, a pass along the shaft, the assembly with a running count, a look down the suction bore, and the painted pump at the end.

Cutaway · 16 seconds

Inside the glazing

A laminated glass sample opens into its three layers, holds while each is named, and closes again. The labels stay pinned to the glass as it turns.

Changeover · 15 seconds

Two faces, one mallet

Both faces unscrew, hold apart with their studs showing, and screw back in. For any product whose selling point is a part that changes.

Photo loop · 18 seconds

Three photographs, one loop

A cartridge, a joint in stone and the finished façade. Approved photographs become moving footage, with your wording and stand number on every shot.

Material study · 16 seconds

Stone, under a moving light

Three finishes, each crossed by a low lamp so the surface shows its relief. The relief is worked out from the photograph itself.

Range with callouts · 16 seconds

Two products, three facts each

Each product arrives with its figures and three callouts that draw on, pinned to the right part.

LED ribbon · 2560 × 720 · 16 seconds

One pendant, three beams

Three optics across a 32:9 canvas. Each beam opens and closes in turn, with its angle shown beneath it.

Portrait totem · 1080 × 1920 · 15 seconds

A sign in two scripts

Arabic and English directions share one portrait totem, with the Arabic set right to left and the arrow pointing the way.
